## Key discrepancies (key appears outdated or wrong versus current sources)

- CARD-0133 (What types of insurances companies are non-admitted and have no rate r): The key calls county mutuals, farm mutuals, reciprocals, Lloyd's plans, RRGs and purchasing groups 'non-admitted/no rate regulation.' County mutuals, Lloyd's and reciprocals are licensed Texas insurers, and since 2003 Ins. Code §2251.003 applies rate regulation to Lloyd's plans, reciprocal exchanges and county mutuals for most P&C lines.
- CARD-0157 (Comparative Negligence): Key says recovery allowed only if negligence is 49% or less; Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §33.001 bars recovery only if claimant is MORE than 50% responsible (51% bar), so 50% still recovers.
- CARD-0161 (Texas Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act (1982)): The key's $85,000 cash deposit / $85,000 CSL is outdated: the minimum limits are 30/60/25 (Transp. Code §601.072) and the cash/securities deposit is $55,000 (§601.122).
- CARD-0203 (Texas Dwelling policy): The key says the dwelling policy can't be used 'within 500 feet of business operations'; a Texas prelicensing manual states the rule as incidental business use not exceeding 500 square feet or 50% of floor space, so the key appears to garble it.
- CARD-0208 (What are the coverages and valuation for Basic DP1 under Texas Dwellin): The key's DP-1 structure (A dwelling, B other structures, C contents, D FRV 20% of A) doesn't match the current TDI TDP-1 form: Coverage A Dwelling, Coverage B Personal Property, other structures as a 10% extension, ACV settlement, and FRV only 'if applicable.'
- CARD-0209 (What are the coverages and valuation Broad DP2 of Texas Dwelling Polic): The key says DP-2 settles buildings at replacement value; the current TDI TDP-2 form settles at actual cash value (replacement cost appears on TDP-3).
- CARD-0224 (What are the coverages and valuation for HO-2 under Texas Homeowner Po): Key says HO-2 dwelling/other structures settle at ACV; the ISO HO 00 02 form settles buildings at replacement cost if insured to 80% of replacement cost.
- CARD-0242 (What are supplemental payments for Commercial General Liability (CGL)?): Key says CGL supplementary payments include loss of earnings up to $100/day; the current ISO CG 00 01 form pays up to $250/day (key reflects an older edition).
- CARD-0248 (Premium audit): The key's 'premium audit' definition (investigate premises conditions) describes the inspections condition; the audit condition is examination of the insured's books and records to compute final premium.
- CARD-0275 (What is a legal beneficiary under Texas Workers Compensation?): Key says legal beneficiaries are 'spouses and children only'; Tex. Labor Code §401.011(28) and §408.182-.183 make dependent grandchildren, parents, siblings, and others eligible for death benefits too.
- CARD-0284 (What is the waiting period for Texas Worker’s Compensation?): Key says benefits become retroactive to day one if disability lasts 4 weeks; Tex. Labor Code §408.082 makes them retroactive if disability lasts 2 weeks or longer.
- CARD-0287 (What is Average Weekly Wage under Texas Workers Compensation?): Key says AWW averages the last 12 weeks; Tex. Labor Code §408.041 uses the 13 consecutive weeks before the injury.
- CARD-0302 (What are the required amount of CE credit and in what timeframe?): Key says 30 CE hours every 2 years; TDI rules (28 TAC §19.1003) require adjusters to complete 24 hours, including 2 hours of ethics, each 2-year license period.
- CARD-0311 (Deceptive Trade Practices Act, what is it unlawful for a consumer to d): Key ('drop charges filed against agent unless they have $5,000,000 in assets') garbles the DTPA; Bus. & Com. Code §17.45(4) excludes business consumers with assets of $25 million or more from 'consumer' status.
- CARD-0339 (What is the difference between HO2 valuation and HO3/HO5?): Same as 0224: ISO HO-2 settles the dwelling at replacement cost (80% rule), not ACV.
- CARD-0359 (What is the timeframe in order to have Boiler and Machinery paid out i): The key says 18 months; the current ISO EB 00 20 equipment breakdown form allows 24 months to repair/replace before limiting payment to ACV.
- CARD-0361 (What is the basic deductible for Commercial Property?): Same as 0592: the $250 commercial property deductible is outdated; ISO's current standard is $500.
- CARD-0370 (What are the coverage extensions for Commercial Property policies?): The key's amounts (30 days, $250 per tree, glass $100/$500) come from older forms; the current CP 00 10 uses 180 days for newly acquired property and higher outdoor-property sublimits.
- CARD-0390 (When must a licensed agent report a change of address?): The key says 'immediately'; TDI form FIN533 requires agents/adjusters to report a name or address change within 30 days.
- CARD-0452 (What is Part 1 of Texas Workers Compensation): Key reduces Part One to 'the medical coverage'; under the standard WC & EL policy (WC 00 00 00), Part One pays all benefits the state WC law requires: medical, income and death benefits.
- CARD-0459 (What are the coverages on TDP?): The key's TDP structure/valuations (DP-2 replacement cost; FRV 20% on DP-1) don't match the current TDI TDP forms (Coverage B = personal property; TDP-1/TDP-2 ACV; TDP-3 replacement cost and loss of use 20% of A).
- CARD-0460 (What does Coverage A on TDP cover?): The key says DP-2 is replacement cost; the current TDI TDP-2 form settles at ACV.
- CARD-0461 (What does Coverage B of TDP cover?): The key says DP-2 is replacement cost; the current TDI TDP-2 form settles at ACV (the 10%-of-A limit matches).
- CARD-0592 (What is the deductible for Commercial Property Policy?): The $250 commercial property deductible in the key is outdated; current ISO commercial property/businessowners rating uses a $500 standard deductible.

Also flagged but graded correct because my answer included the deck's figure: 0324 and 0343. The deck's 30-day PAP nonrenewal notice is outdated; Ins. Code §551.105 now requires notice by the 60th day before expiration.

## Unsupported (key detail could not be verified in a real, non-flashcard source)

- CARD-0110 (Binder): The deck's '30 days, rarely more than 60' binder duration: no Texas source found (Ins. Code §549.055 only requires a lender-accepted binder to be replaced within 30 days).
- CARD-0153 (Actual Damages): The deck's insurance-specific definition of 'actual damages' (reduction in value of property by an insured peril; amount claimed/paid): no authoritative source found; only the general legal meaning (compensatory/economic damages) is sourced.
- CARD-0217 (What is the Texas Homeowner Policy?): Texas homeowners eligibility limits in the key (no more than 50% business use, not within 500 feet of a business, no mobile homes): not found in an accessible TDI/manual source; only the 'own and occupy / reside within 60 days' rule is sourced.
- CARD-0265 (Extensions on Section 1 (property) for TBOP): TBOP extension amounts in the key (outdoor property incl. signs $2,500; trees $500 each): not found; only the extensions themselves are sourced (ISO BP 00 03).
- CARD-0271 (What is Section D under TBOP?): TBOP medical expense limit of $5,000 per person: only an analogous CGL example ($5,000 medical expense limit, IRMI) was found; not verified for TBOP.
- CARD-0308 (What can only a PCA do?): 'Only a PCA can sign a policy': only a general definition of countersignature laws (IRMI) was found; no current Texas source confirms it.
- CARD-0590 (Commercial Package Policy common policy declarations): The key's description of CPP common policy declarations (covered property, primary/optional coverages, limits, perils, mortgagee, deductible): only a generic 'declarations' reference was found.
