# Study plan

Reading order for the 597-card Texas all-lines adjuster deck. One section at a time. The pace below is a suggestion. Stretch a long section across two sittings, or pair two short ones, whenever the self-check tells you to.

## Where the answers come from

Round 1 of the sourced pass scored 511 of 597 against the key. The final pass is 566 cards that match the key with a citation, 24 key discrepancies, and 7 unsupported cards. A key discrepancy means the sourced answer follows the current Texas source and the key looks outdated. An unsupported card means the key's specific detail was not found in a real source. The research write-up is [`reports/summary.md`](reports/summary.md).

Study the sourced answer on a discrepancy. The original key, whose header says the answers are unverified, is [`data/answer_key.txt`](data/answer_key.txt). Sourced answers and the citation for each card are in [`data/final_answers.csv`](data/final_answers.csv).

Texas sources are preferred. Mike Russ, the California Department of Insurance commercial guide, IRMI, Cornell Wex, and Wikipedia are marked general-concept only in the section files. ISO and other national forms are listed when the card asks about that form, with a note that the Texas form controls on Texas-specific cards.

## Why this order

The glossary and the contract come first so the forms have words. Negligence comes next because liability coverage is a promise to pay for it. Personal property and the Texas dwelling forms come before auto, then commercial property and the Texas businessowners policy, then the CGL and the other liability policies. Workers' compensation is its own code, so it gets its own day after the liability form. Surety, crime, marine, flood, and equipment breakdown are shorter and sit once the big forms are in place. Licensing and claims handling close the sequence: the statutes assume you already know what a policy says.

The original decks are not the study order. `card sharp qa.txt` is mostly section 1. `ch6-10.txt` walks dwelling, homeowners, commercial property, the CGL, the businessowners policy, workers' compensation, and bonds. `review.txt` and `100 questions.txt` mix every topic, so those cards are filed by subject in the section files.

## How to use a section file

Each file in [`reading/`](reading/) lists the card IDs, the reading, the key discrepancies and unsupported cards, and a self-check. Read the Texas sources before the general-concept ones. For a long PDF, the file names the part to read (a coverage, a part, or a statute section). Page numbers are not invented where the research did not give one.

On a **key discrepancy**, learn the sourced answer. The key's version is recorded in the note so you can see what the deck said. On an **unsupported** card, the note tells you which piece of the key could not be found. Do not fill that gap from memory.
