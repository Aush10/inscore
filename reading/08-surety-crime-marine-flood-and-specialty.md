## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

### CARD-0359 — Key discrepancy

*What is the timeframe in order to have Boiler and Machinery paid out in replacement value?*

The key says 18 months; the current ISO EB 00 20 equipment breakdown form allows 24 months to repair/replace before limiting payment to ACV.

Sourced answer: Under the current ISO Equipment Breakdown form (EB 00 20), repair or replacement must happen within 24 months after the breakdown or the insurer pays only the smaller of repair/replacement cost or ACV. The deck's 18-month figure reflects older forms.
