## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

### CARD-0157 — Key discrepancy

*Comparative Negligence*

Key says recovery allowed only if negligence is 49% or less; Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code §33.001 bars recovery only if claimant is MORE than 50% responsible (51% bar), so 50% still recovers.

Sourced answer: Damages apportioned by percentage of fault; Texas modified comparative (51% bar) — recovery reduced by own percentage, barred if over 50%.

### CARD-0153 — Unsupported

*Actual Damages*

The deck's insurance-specific definition of 'actual damages' (reduction in value of property by an insured peril; amount claimed/paid): no authoritative source found; only the general legal meaning (compensatory/economic damages) is sourced.

Sourced answer: In the deck's insurance sense: the actual reduction in value of the insured's property caused by an insured peril, i.e., the amount sought in a claim or paid on the insured's behalf. Legally, actual (compensatory) damages compensate the claimant's real economic loss.
