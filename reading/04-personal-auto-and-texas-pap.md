## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

- Medical payments at $500 (CARD-0179, CARD-0195, CARD-0358) is backed only by a Texas law-firm handout that lists $500 as the lowest limit option. The research summary flags that dollar figure as thinly sourced.

- CARD-0324 and CARD-0343 were graded correct because the sourced answer included the deck's 30-day nonrenewal figure. Insurance Code §551.105 now requires the insurer to give nonrenewal notice by the 60th day before expiration. Learn the 60-day rule.

### CARD-0161 — Key discrepancy

*Texas Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act (1982)*

The key's $85,000 cash deposit / $85,000 CSL is outdated: the minimum limits are 30/60/25 (Transp. Code §601.072) and the cash/securities deposit is $55,000 (§601.122).

Sourced answer: Tex. Transp. Code ch. 601 requires motor vehicle owners/operators to maintain and show proof of financial responsibility. Minimum limits are 30/60/25 (since 2011). Alternatives: a surety bond, a deposit with the comptroller (now $55,000; the deck's $85,000 is the old figure), or a self-insurance certificate (25+ vehicles). Insurers issue ID cards as proof.

### CARD-0324 — Outdated figure in the key

*Explain PAP 10/10/30?*

Key's PAP '30 days notice for nonrenewal' is outdated; Tex. Ins. Code §551.105 (amended 2023) requires personal auto and residential nonrenewal notice by the 60th day before expiration.

Sourced answer: PAP notice rule: 10 days' notice to cancel for nonpayment, 10 days' notice to cancel for other permitted reasons (incl. during first 60 days), and 30 days' notice of nonrenewal (NOTE: Texas Ins. Code §551.105 now requires nonrenewal notice by the 60th day before expiration).

### CARD-0343 — Outdated figure in the key

*How much notice must be given to a non-renewal on a PAP?*

Same issue as 0324: the 30-day PAP nonrenewal notice is outdated; Tex. Ins. Code §551.105 now requires notice by the 60th day before expiration.

Sourced answer: 30 days' notice in the deck (but current Ins. Code §551.105 requires the insurer to mail nonrenewal notice not later than the 60th day before expiration).

### Other sourced Texas figures on cards graded correct

These matched the key and a citation. They are here because they are easy to mis-memorize. The full wording is in `data/final_answers.csv`.

- `CARD-0163` Texas Automobile Insurance Plan Association — TAIPA is Texas's assigned-risk plan for drivers who, in good faith, are entitled to auto liability insurance but can't get it in the voluntary market. Applicants must certify they were rejected by at least two insurers within 60 days before applying. Coverage is at basic limits (...
- `CARD-0179` Medical Payments Coverage — Pays reasonable medical and funeral expenses for the insured, family members and passengers (occupying a vehicle, or a pedestrian struck by one), regardless of fault, for expenses incurred within 3 years of the accident. Optional in Texas, per person; limits start at $500.
- `CARD-0180` What does Personal Injury Protection (PIP) provide? — No-fault benefits for the insured, household members and passengers: 80% of lost income, medical and funeral expenses, and replacement services for non-income producers; Texas minimum $2,500 per person; must be offered and can only be rejected in writing.
- `CARD-0199` What is the deductible for Uninsured Motorist? — $250 deductible for uninsured motorist property damage (Texas UMPD); no deductible on UM bodily injury.
- `CARD-0194` Where would you find the coverage extension Transportation Expenses? — Under Comprehensive (Other Than Collision) in Part D: the Texas PAP pays up to $20/day, $600 max, for transportation expenses after the total theft of your covered auto, beginning 48 hours after the theft.
- `CARD-0345` What is TE and what are the max amount payable for expenses? — Transportation Expenses (PAP Part D): pays temporary transportation (rental) expenses — after a covered theft beginning 48 hours after theft (up to $20/day, max $600 in ISO PAP; Texas per policy), and loss of use of non-owned autos.
- `CARD-0193` What coverages are only available as an endorsement to BAP? — In Texas, PIP and Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists coverage are added to a Business Auto Policy by endorsement (TE 04 01C PIP; TE 04 09D UM/UIM). Texas law requires both to be provided unless rejected in writing.
- `CARD-0429` What are two optional coverages obtained by endorsement to a BAP? — PIP and Uninsured/Underinsured Motorists (added in Texas by endorsements TE 04 01C and TE 04 09D).
