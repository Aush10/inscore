## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

### CARD-0242 — Key discrepancy

*What are supplemental payments for Commercial General Liability (CGL)?*

Key says CGL supplementary payments include loss of earnings up to $100/day; the current ISO CG 00 01 form pays up to $250/day (key reflects an older edition).

Sourced answer: Paid in addition to limits: all defense expenses, up to $250 for bail bonds, cost of appeal and release-of-attachment bonds, reasonable expenses insured incurs at insurer's request including up to $250/day lost earnings, court costs taxed against insured, pre- and post-judgment interest.

### CARD-0248 — Key discrepancy

*Premium audit*

The key's 'premium audit' definition (investigate premises conditions) describes the inspections condition; the audit condition is examination of the insured's books and records to compute final premium.

Sourced answer: Premium is estimated at inception. The insurer may examine and audit the insured's books and records (payroll, sales) during the policy period and up to 3 years after, and adjusts the final premium. The deck's description (inspections and adjusting the premium when conditions warrant) mixes the audit condition with the inspection condition.
