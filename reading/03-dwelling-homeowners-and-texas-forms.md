# 03. Dwelling, homeowners, and the Texas forms

**92 cards.** Two sittings. First the standard fire policy and the Texas dwelling forms. Then homeowners, including Texas HO-B.

Source decks: 40 from ch6-10.txt, 33 from review.txt, 18 from 100 questions.txt, 1 from card sharp qa.txt.

The fire cards are graded against the standard fire policy. The public text in the citation list is New York's statute (with a Maine copy of the same form). That is not a Texas policy. The Texas cards are the TDI dwelling forms (TDP-1, TDP-2, TDP-3), the Texas HO-B and HO-A forms, and the Texas valued-policy statute. ISO homeowners forms are national. Read them for the HO-2, HO-3, HO-4, HO-5, HO-6, and HO-8 questions. Where a card is about Texas wind, a Texas deductible, or Texas eligibility, use the TDI form, the TDI guide, or the Texas rating manual instead.

## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

- CARD-0211 was graded correct. Its sourced answer says indirect loss on the dwelling forms is limited to 20% of Coverage A, with 10% fair rental value on the basic form. CARD-0208 and CARD-0459 are key discrepancies: the current TDI forms do not use the key's Coverage A/B/C/D layout, and fair rental value on TDP-1 is 'if applicable.' When those two descriptions conflict, follow the discrepancy cards and the TDP forms.

- CARD-0086, in section 1, is the valued-contract definition. It already states the Texas total-fire-loss rule. The cards to quiz for that rule are CARD-0383 and CARD-0521 in this section.

### CARD-0203 — Key discrepancy

*Texas Dwelling policy*

The key says the dwelling policy can't be used 'within 500 feet of business operations'; a Texas prelicensing manual states the rule as incidental business use not exceeding 500 square feet or 50% of floor space, so the key appears to garble it.

Sourced answer: A TDI-promulgated property-only policy for 1-4 family dwellings. The owner must have an insurable interest but need not occupy the home (rentals, vacant homes). No liability or theft of personal property unless endorsed. Incidental business use is allowed only if it takes no more than 500 square feet or 50% of the floor space, whichever is less (the deck's '500 feet from business operations' appears to garble this rule). Per the deck it is not for houseboats, boathouses, condos or dwellings under construction.

### CARD-0208 — Key discrepancy

*What are the coverages and valuation for Basic DP1 under Texas Dwelling Policy?*

The key's DP-1 structure (A dwelling, B other structures, C contents, D FRV 20% of A) doesn't match the current TDI TDP-1 form: Coverage A Dwelling, Coverage B Personal Property, other structures as a 10% extension, ACV settlement, and FRV only 'if applicable.'

Sourced answer: Per the deck: A Dwelling, B Other Structures and C Personal Property at ACV, with D Fair Rental Value up to 20% of Coverage A. On the actual TDI TDP-1 form, Coverage A Dwelling and Coverage B Personal Property settle at actual cash value (depreciation deducted), other structures are 10% of Coverage A, and fair rental value applies 'if applicable' (endorsement).

### CARD-0209 — Key discrepancy

*What are the coverages and valuation Broad DP2 of Texas Dwelling Policy?*

The key says DP-2 settles buildings at replacement value; the current TDI TDP-2 form settles at actual cash value (replacement cost appears on TDP-3).

Sourced answer: Per the deck: A and B at replacement value, C at ACV, D and E up to 20% of Coverage A. The current TDI TDP-2 form actually settles losses at actual cash value; replacement cost and the 20%-of-Coverage-A loss-of-use limit appear on the TDP-3 form.

### CARD-0459 — Key discrepancy

*What are the coverages on TDP?*

The key's TDP structure/valuations (DP-2 replacement cost; FRV 20% on DP-1) don't match the current TDI TDP forms (Coverage B = personal property; TDP-1/TDP-2 ACV; TDP-3 replacement cost and loss of use 20% of A).

Sourced answer: Per the deck: A Dwelling, B Other Structures, C Personal Property, D Fair Rental Value, E ALE (DP-1 ACV; DP-2/DP-3 replacement; loss of use 20% of A). On the actual TDI forms: Coverage A Dwelling, Coverage B Personal Property, other structures as an extension of 10% of A, loss of use (FRV + ALE) 20% of A on TDP-3; TDP-1/TDP-2 settle at ACV and TDP-3 at replacement cost.

### CARD-0460 — Key discrepancy

*What does Coverage A on TDP cover?*

The key says DP-2 is replacement cost; the current TDI TDP-2 form settles at ACV.

Sourced answer: The dwelling (and attached structures), materials and supplies on premises for repairs, and building equipment/appliances provided by the landlord. TDP-1 (and the current TDP-2) at ACV; TDP-3 at replacement cost.

### CARD-0461 — Key discrepancy

*What does Coverage B of TDP cover?*

The key says DP-2 is replacement cost; the current TDI TDP-2 form settles at ACV (the 10%-of-A limit matches).

Sourced answer: Detached (other) structures separated by clear space, limited to 10% of Coverage A. ACV on TDP-1 (and the current TDP-2); replacement cost on TDP-3.

*What is the Texas Homeowner Policy?*

Texas homeowners eligibility limits in the key (no more than 50% business use, not within 500 feet of a business, no mobile homes): not found in an accessible TDI/manual source; only the 'own and occupy / reside within 60 days' rule is sourced.

Sourced answer: A package policy (property plus liability) for owner-occupied homes. The applicant must own and occupy the dwelling, or intend to reside there within 60 days. Per the deck's rules: no more than 50% business use, not within 500 feet of a business, and not for mobile homes.

### CARD-0224 — Key discrepancy

*What are the coverages and valuation for HO-2 under Texas Homeowner Policy?*

Key says HO-2 dwelling/other structures settle at ACV; the ISO HO 00 02 form settles buildings at replacement cost if insured to 80% of replacement cost.

Sourced answer: Coverages A-F. Per the deck: A and B at ACV, C at ACV, D 20% of A, E $100,000, F $1,000. The ISO HO 00 02 form actually settles dwelling/other structures at replacement cost if insured to 80%; personal property is at ACV.

### CARD-0339 — Key discrepancy

*What is the difference between HO2 valuation and HO3/HO5?*

Same as 0224: ISO HO-2 settles the dwelling at replacement cost (80% rule), not ACV.

Sourced answer: Per the deck: HO-2 is ACV; HO-3/HO-5 are replacement cost. Note: the ISO HO 00 02 form also pays replacement cost on buildings when insured to at least 80% (ACV dwelling settlement is an HO-8 or Texas HO-A feature).
