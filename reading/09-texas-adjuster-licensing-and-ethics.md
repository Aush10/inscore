# 09. Texas adjuster licensing, regulation, and ethics

**41 cards.** One sitting. This is statute reading more than form reading.

Source decks: 28 from review.txt, 13 from 100 questions.txt.

Insurance Code chapter 4101 is the adjuster license. Chapter 4004 is continuing education. Chapter 4003 is expiration and renewal. The ethics cards are the unfair trade practices in chapter 541 that deal with how a license holder behaves: twisting, rebating, defamation, and misrepresentation of a policy. Claim-settlement deadlines and the DTPA are the next section, so they are not repeated here. Insurer domicile (domestic, foreign, alien) and the guaranty association are in this section. Surplus lines and company types that the glossary introduced are back in section 1 if you want the longer market list.

## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

### CARD-0302 — Key discrepancy

*What are the required amount of CE credit and in what timeframe?*

Key says 30 CE hours every 2 years; TDI rules (28 TAC §19.1003) require adjusters to complete 24 hours, including 2 hours of ethics, each 2-year license period.

Sourced answer: Texas adjusters: 24 hours of CE every 2-year license period, including at least 2 hours of ethics; at least 50% in a classroom or classroom-equivalent setting; completed before license expiration.

### CARD-0308 — Unsupported

*What can only a PCA do?*

'Only a PCA can sign a policy': only a general definition of countersignature laws (IRMI) was found; no current Texas source confirms it.

Sourced answer: Sign (countersign) a policy on behalf of the insurer: only a licensed, appointed property & casualty agent (PCA) may do so, not an adjuster. Countersignature laws historically required policies to be signed by an agent residing in the state.

### CARD-0390 — Key discrepancy

*When must a licensed agent report a change of address?*

The key says 'immediately'; TDI form FIN533 requires agents/adjusters to report a name or address change within 30 days.

Sourced answer: Within 30 days of the change (TDI form FIN533). The deck says 'immediately.'

- `CARD-0550` Which Texas code chapter governs the licensing of insurance adjusters? — Texas Insurance Code Chapter 4101 (Insurance Adjusters).
- `CARD-0553` A person training under a licensed adjuster's supervision registers as a trainee. For how  — Up to 12 months (if registered with TDI as a trainee at the start).
- `CARD-0313` If an agent knowingly commits Unfair Trade Practices, the damages may be — Up to three times actual (economic) damages (treble damages) plus attorney's fees under Ins. Code ch. 541 / DTPA.
