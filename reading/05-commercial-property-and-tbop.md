## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

### CARD-0361 — Key discrepancy

*What is the basic deductible for Commercial Property?*

Same as 0592: the $250 commercial property deductible is outdated; ISO's current standard is $500.

Sourced answer: The deck says $250; current ISO commercial property/businessowners rating uses a $500 standard deductible.

### CARD-0370 — Key discrepancy

*What are the coverage extensions for Commercial Property policies?*

The key's amounts (30 days, $250 per tree, glass $100/$500) come from older forms; the current CP 00 10 uses 180 days for newly acquired property and higher outdoor-property sublimits.

Sourced answer: Newly acquired buildings and newly acquired personal property; personal property off premises; personal effects and property of others; valuable papers and records; outdoor property (fences, signs, trees/shrubs/plants with per-item sublimits); non-owned detached trailers. The deck's amounts ($250 per tree, glass $100/$500, 30 days) are from older forms; current CP 00 10 uses 180 days and $1,000 per tree.

### CARD-0590 — Unsupported

*Commercial Package Policy common policy declarations*

The key's description of CPP common policy declarations (covered property, primary/optional coverages, limits, perils, mortgagee, deductible): only a generic 'declarations' reference was found.

Sourced answer: Shows the named insured, address and policy period, and describes the covered property/coverage parts, designating primary and optional coverages, limits, perils (causes of loss), mortgagee and deductible information.

### CARD-0592 — Key discrepancy

*What is the deductible for Commercial Property Policy?*

The $250 commercial property deductible in the key is outdated; current ISO commercial property/businessowners rating uses a $500 standard deductible.

Sourced answer: The deck says $250; current ISO commercial property/businessowners rating uses a $500 standard deductible.

### CARD-0265 — Unsupported

*Extensions on Section 1 (property) for TBOP*

TBOP extension amounts in the key (outdoor property incl. signs $2,500; trees $500 each): not found; only the extensions themselves are sourced (ISO BP 00 03).

Sourced answer: Additional amounts of insurance for: buildings and business personal property at newly acquired premises; personal property off premises; outdoor property, including signs (deck: $2,500, with trees/shrubs/plants $500 each); and valuable papers and records/electronic media.

### CARD-0271 — Unsupported

*What is Section D under TBOP?*

TBOP medical expense limit of $5,000 per person: only an analogous CGL example ($5,000 medical expense limit, IRMI) was found; not verified for TBOP.

Sourced answer: Coverage D, Medical Expenses: paid without regard to fault for injuries from an accident on premises or from operations, with expenses reported within 1 year (deck: $5,000 per person).
