## Key facts to watch

Discrepancies and unsupported cards are quoted from the research notes and the sourced answer. The note says what the key got wrong. The sourced answer is the version to learn.

- The California course's state-fund discussion was removed from CARD-0457. Use TDI Bulletin B-0035-99 and Texas Mutual's history page for the Texas Workers' Compensation Insurance Fund.

### CARD-0275 — Key discrepancy

*What is a legal beneficiary under Texas Workers Compensation?*

Key says legal beneficiaries are 'spouses and children only'; Tex. Labor Code §401.011(28) and §408.182-.183 make dependent grandchildren, parents, siblings, and others eligible for death benefits too.

Sourced answer: A person entitled to receive death benefits — eligible spouse, children/grandchildren, dependent parents, siblings, etc., as defined by the Labor Code.

### CARD-0284 — Key discrepancy

*What is the waiting period for Texas Worker’s Compensation?*

Key says benefits become retroactive to day one if disability lasts 4 weeks; Tex. Labor Code §408.082 makes them retroactive if disability lasts 2 weeks or longer.

Sourced answer: 7 days — income benefits begin to accrue on the 8th day of disability; if disability lasts 2 weeks or more, benefits are paid back to the first day.

### CARD-0287 — Key discrepancy

*What is Average Weekly Wage under Texas Workers Compensation?*

Key says AWW averages the last 12 weeks; Tex. Labor Code §408.041 uses the 13 consecutive weeks before the injury.

Sourced answer: Generally the sum of wages paid in the 13 consecutive weeks before the injury divided by 13 (or wage of a similar employee if employed less than 13 weeks).

### CARD-0452 — Key discrepancy

*What is Part 1 of Texas Workers Compensation*

Key reduces Part One to 'the medical coverage'; under the standard WC & EL policy (WC 00 00 00), Part One pays all benefits the state WC law requires: medical, income and death benefits.

Sourced answer: Part One – Workers' Compensation Insurance: pays the benefits required by the workers' comp law of states listed in Item 3.A.

### Other sourced Texas figures on cards graded correct

These matched the key and a citation. They are here because they are easy to mis-memorize. The full wording is in `data/final_answers.csv`.

- `CARD-0272` What code is the Texas workers compensation established under? — Texas Labor Code, Title 5, Subtitle A (Texas Workers' Compensation Act), administered by TDI-Division of Workers' Compensation.
- `CARD-0282` Can an employee reject workers compensation ?w — Yes — in Texas an employee may waive coverage and retain common-law rights by notifying the employer in writing within 5 days after hire (or after employer obtains coverage).
- `CARD-0456` Premiums on Texas Workers Compensation are — Based on payroll per $100 of payroll by job classification, adjusted for experience modification (rates regulated/filed with TDI); estimated and audited.
- `CARD-0457` Texas Workers Compensation Fund — Created in 1991 as a state-chartered competitive workers' comp insurer and insurer of last resort (taking good and bad risks), initially financed by revenue bonds issued through the Texas Public Finance Authority; converted to Texas Mutual Insurance Company in 2001.
